Personal Statement

Persistence and Analytical Thinking Developed as a Wheelchair User

I have used a wheelchair since I was three years old. To overcome physical limitations, I developed the habit of thinking carefully and finding efficient ways to do things.

Computers opened up a world of possibilities for me. With limitations on physical activity, I became absorbed in understanding the logical structures on screen and identifying errors. Through my studies in AI Semiconductor Convergence and the advanced Computer Engineering program, I developed an understanding of how hardware and software interact.

Because even a single physical movement required careful consideration, I developed a cautious approach to anticipating and preventing errors in programming. I believe this persistence and analytical thinking will serve me well in QA, where tracking down bugs requires sustained attention.

Taking the First Step Toward a Career in QA

I believe quality assurance is essential to delivering valuable IT services to customers. Drawing on my understanding of the software life cycle from my Computer Engineering studies, I can work beyond basic testing to design and verify possible usage scenarios.

When I join a team, I will focus on two priorities. The first is clear communication. I will use the documentation skills I developed through qualifications such as ITQ to write structured test cases and clearly communicate defects to the development team.

The second is a user-centered perspective. The difficulties I encounter in daily life as a wheelchair user have helped me recognize what makes a service convenient and accessible to a wider range of people.

Strengths and Areas for Development

My interest in many fields sometimes leaves me feeling that I need greater depth in a particular technology. I address this through rapid learning and detailed documentation.

When I encounter an unfamiliar field or a new tool, I read the manuals carefully, organize the key concepts in my own words, and archive my notes. This habit helps me learn work processes quickly in new environments.

After joining a team, I will ask honest questions about what I do not know and continue investigating until I understand it well. I will keep building deeper professional expertise.

Learning During My Student Years

Although my grades were initially low, I attended classes diligently and worked to improve my understanding. For subjects I found difficult, I created separate study notes and reviewed them repeatedly, gradually improving my grades. This experience helped me develop consistent study habits and self-management skills.

Career Goals
